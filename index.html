About Me

Connecting robust backend architecture with responsive, dynamic cross-platform user experiences.

I'm a Full Stack Developer at Harvee Technologies and a 2026 B.E. Computer Science & Engineering graduate from Hindusthan Institute of Technology. My engineering practice sits at the convergence of resilient backend architecture, healthcare & enterprise platforms (ZureSmile), cross-platform mobile apps (DSM Academy / Flutter), WordPress CMS & PHP solutions, and cryptographic cybersecurity.
